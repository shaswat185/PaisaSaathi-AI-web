Subject to the information actually collected and stored by the app, an approved deletion request may cover:

  • Saved preferences and other account-linked information.
  • Stored chat history or AI interaction records associated with your account, where applicable.

The exact data covered by a request depends on the features you use and the systems in which your information is stored.

3. Data Retention and Legal Exceptions

We will assess deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy. Certain information may need to be retained for a legally required period or where retention is otherwise permitted by law, such as for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance obligations.

Where information must be retained, it will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant retention practices. Data that is no longer required should be deleted or de-identified where appropriate and technically feasible.

4. Processing Time

We aim to acknowledge and process valid requests as soon as reasonably possible. The time required may depend on account verification, the systems involved, and any applicable legal requirements. If processing is delayed, we may communicate the reason and any relevant next steps.

A deletion request is not confirmation that deletion has already been completed. We will not represent a request as completed until the applicable deletion process has been carried out.
