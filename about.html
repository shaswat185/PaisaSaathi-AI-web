About PaisaSaathi AI

A friendly, Hinglish-first companion that makes budgeting and personal-finance basics easier to understand.

Our mission

Most people in India learn about money by trial and error. PaisaSaathi AI exists to make the basics — budgeting, saving, understanding everyday financial terms — easier to follow, in the Hinglish we actually speak.

What PaisaSaathi AI does

  • Helps you think through a monthly budget: income, essentials, spending and savings goals.
  • Lets you ask general personal-finance questions and get plain-language, educational answers.
  • Encourages small, realistic habits rather than quick-rich promises.

What it does not do

  • It is not a bank, lender, broker, insurance intermediary, tax adviser or registered investment adviser.
  • It does not give personalised investment, tax or legal advice, and never guarantees returns.
  • It never asks for banking passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or card details.

How we think about trust

We explain what data the app processes in our Privacy Policy, keep our Terms and Disclaimer in plain language, and provide a clear way to request account deletion. AI can make mistakes, so we encourage you to verify important information with reliable sources.

Who is behind it

PaisaSaathi AI is built and maintained by an independent developer from Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, India. Feedback is very welcome — it directly shapes what gets built next.
